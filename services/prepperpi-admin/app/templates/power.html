Shut down or restart

Always shut down this way before unplugging the Pi. Pulling the power while it's running can corrupt the SD card — most often mid-download, when a content file is being written.

Halts cleanly, then it's safe to unplug.
Comes back on its own in about a minute.

Physical power button

Wire a momentary push-button across two pins on the Pi's 40-pin header and a short press shuts the Pi down cleanly — no phone, no browser. This is handled by the Pi's own firmware and Linux, not by PrepperPi, so the button still works even if PrepperPi's services have stopped.

Two things worth knowing:
  • GPIO3 (header pin 5) also turns the Pi back on. Pressing the same button while the Pi is halted boots it again, so one button does both. The other pins only shut down.
  • GPIO3 is shared with I²C. If you've added a real-time clock or any other I²C board on header pins 3 and 5, pick a different pin here — otherwise normal I²C traffic will look like button presses and shut the Pi down at random.
Takes effect after the next restart.
