Shutting down…

PrepperPi is shutting down now. This takes about 20 seconds.

  1. Watch the green light on the Pi. It will flicker while the Pi finishes writing to the SD card.
  2. When the green light stops blinking and stays off, the Pi has halted. Only the red power light will still be lit.
  3. It is now safe to unplug the power.

This page won't update — the Pi is going away. The Wi-Fi network will disappear in a few seconds, which is the other sign that it worked.

To start it up again, plug the power back in. It takes two to three minutes before the PrepperPi Wi-Fi network reappears.

Restarting…

PrepperPi is restarting now.

  1. The PrepperPi Wi-Fi network will disappear shortly.
  2. Wait two to three minutes, then reconnect to it from your Wi-Fi settings.
  3. Open any web address and the portal will come back.

This page won't update on its own — the Pi is restarting. Don't unplug it while it's coming back up.
